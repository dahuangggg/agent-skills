# Learner Profile

`LEARNER-PROFILE.md` is a revisable assessment, not an activity log and not proof that generated material was learned.

## Template

```md
# Learner Profile: {topic}

Updated: {date and triggering lesson/diagnostic}

## Current level
{Concise assessment and confidence: low, medium, or high.}

## Demonstrated strengths
- {Capability} — Evidence: {answer or completed task}

## Gaps and misconceptions
- {Gap or misconception} — Evidence: {answer}; Confidence: {level}

## Appropriate challenge
{What is difficult enough to teach next without exceeding working memory.}

## Recommended starting point / next step
{Lesson and reason.}

## Uncertainties
- {What has not yet been tested}
```

Create the initial profile from the pre-course diagnostic. After that, update it only from observable lesson answers, exercises, repository work, or explicit prior-experience disclosures. Keep diagnostic evidence separate from later demonstrated learning. The profile may guide future course adaptation, but it does not silently rewrite an approved course plan.
